Hey, I’m Yuvvan

I spend most of my time thinking about systems. Not just software systems, but systems that shape the way people think, create, and live. I’m interested in what these systems should do, how they should behave, and the subtle ways they influence the people who interact with them every day.

Some ideas that intrigue me: How should these systems evolve to better complement the way we live? How can great design complement the functionality of these systems? How can we abstract complex, technical, or buried systems into an interface, a product, an interaction?
