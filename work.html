Projects

  • A full RLVR post-training pipeline, built end to end: A policy model, a vLLM-colocated rollout engine, a sandboxed verifier that grades code against real competitive-programming tests, a PPO trainer, and an eval + profiling harness.

    Rollout (vLLM) Verifier (sandbox) PPO Eval

    Through ablations found that rollouts, was the primary bottleneck and optimized for rollout-engine, policy model weight-syncing. Grading on fraction-of-tests-passed instead of pass/fail roughly doubled the reward signal and pushed held-out pass@1 to 2.0% vs. 1.0% for binary reward, after the same # of PPO steps.

  • A Rust user-space kernel that runs untrusted Linux ARM64 binaries natively on macOS, without booting a Linux VM. Every syscall from the guest traps through Apple’s Hypervisor.framework as a hardware VM-exit and is serviced entirely in user space, its own network stack, its own filesystem layer, handled through a separate sandboxed process.
